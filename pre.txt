Manhole Detection ek computer vision system hai jo images ya videos mein open aur damaged manholes ko detect karta hai.
Is project mein YOLOv8 deep learning model ka use karke manholes ko accurately identify kiya jata hai.
Yeh system roads ki monitoring aur public safety ko improve karne mein madad karta hai.
Future mein is technology ko smart cities aur automatic road inspection systems mein use kiya ja sakta hai.
